Roots

 Method with 2 overloads

  • Roots​(AngouriMath.​Functions.​IntegerPolynomial,​PeterO.​Numbers.​EContext)

    Summary

    The roots of squareFree, which has degree at least one and no repeated
    root, at the precision of context; null where they
    are not settled.

  • Roots​(PeterO.​Numbers.​EDecimal[],​PeterO.​Numbers.​EContext,​System.​Int32)

    Summary

    The roots of the polynomial with real coefficients, lowest power first,
    at the precision of context, each approximation at least
    10^-separationDigits of the size of the roots from the others;
    null where they are not settled or not that far apart.

    Remarks

    Solved for w = z / 10^e, with 10^e the size of the roots, so that the
    iteration below meets roots of about unit size whatever theirs is. Its starting circle,
    the tolerance it settles to and the separation it holds the roots to are in absolute
    terms, and a polynomial whose roots were all within 10^-12 of zero was started
    10^12 times as far out as they are and did not settle; with roots within
    10^-30, the products the step divides by passed below the context's exponent
    range and every root came out NaN. The system solved for the parts of x in
    (1 + i) + 2x - 3x^2 + x|3 - x| = 0 with x written as 10^12 x kept one root
    of its two. The scaling is by a power of ten, which is exact.
