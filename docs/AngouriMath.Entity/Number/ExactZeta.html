ExactZeta​(PeterO.​Numbers.​EInteger,​PeterO.​Numbers.​ERational)

 Method (no overloads)

Summary

zeta(s, a) exactly at a whole s and a rational a, or null: NaN at the pole s = 1 and where a is 0, -1, -2, ...; the rational -B_(n + 1)(a)/(n + 1) at s = -n (DLMF 25.11.14); and at s >= 2 with a whole or half-whole a, zeta(s), or
(2^s - 1) zeta(s) at a = 1/2 (DLMF 25.11.11), less the terms that move a there
(25.11.3), where zeta(2m) = (-1)^(m + 1) B_(2m) (2 pi)^(2m)/(2 (2m)!) (25.6.2) and zeta at
an odd number is left as the Riemann zeta function.
