Bound​(``0,​System.​Func{``0,​AngouriMath.​Entity.​Set})

 Method (no overloads)

Summary

The answer solve gives to one equation, with each parameter it brings
for a whole number bound by an indexed union, where no scope is open; as it is given
inside a scope, where it is a step of a system's solve.

Remarks

A family of roots is every value of its expression over the whole numbers, which is the
indexed union union({ pi/6 + 2 pi n, 5 pi/6 + 2 pi n }, n in ZZ). Written with a
free n_1 it was a finite set, which claims to be every root and lists an
expression that is none of them, and nothing said what n_1 ranges over: the
union binds its name and decides membership, 4 pi in union({ 2 pi n }, n in ZZ) being True. A system's rows are a matrix, whose entries cannot bind a name, so inside a
system's scope the parameters stay free and are named with their sets there.
https://github.com/asc-community/AngouriMath/issues/2319
 A parameter over a continuum, the r_1 of abs(x) = 2 or the t_1 of
floor(x) = 1, carries the set it ranges over as a condition on the root already,
and is left as it is.
