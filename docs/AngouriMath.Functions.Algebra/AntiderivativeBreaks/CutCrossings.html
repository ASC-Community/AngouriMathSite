CutCrossings​(AngouriMath.​Entity,​AngouriMath.​Entity.​Variable,​AngouriMath.​Entity.​Number.​Real,​AngouriMath.​Entity.​Number.​Real)

 Method (no overloads)

Summary

The points of [lower, upper] where a node of
antiderivative whose argument leaves the real line crosses its branch cut, each with the
node, the point, its decimal value and the unit its jump there is a whole multiple of; an empty list where no
argument leaves the real line, or none crosses a cut; and null where a crossing cannot be
ruled out or its jump cannot be settled. Bounds that are not numbers are passed as null, and
any crossing at all is then one that cannot be placed.

Remarks

Inside(AngouriMath.Entity,AngouriMath.Entity.Variable,AngouriMath.Entity.Number.Real,AngouriMath.Entity.Number.Real) reads the real line with the parameters real, where arctan and the logarithm of a
real argument have no cut to cross. A complex constant takes the argument off the line, and the path then
can cross a cut where the integrand is continuous: arctan(t - 3i), the antiderivative of
1/(1 + (t - 3i)^2), meets the imaginary axis at -3i, on arctan's cut, and jumps by
pi there, so its values at -2 and 2 differ by pi more than the integral between them does.
https://github.com/asc-community/AngouriMath/issues/2460
 The cuts placed are arctan's, the imaginary axis beyond ±i, and the logarithm's, the negative
real axis: for an argument that is a polynomial with numeric coefficients, where its real or its imaginary
part, a real polynomial, is zero; and for the logarithm of the cosine or the sine of a t + b, which
tan and cot integrate to, where its imaginary part is zero (see
TrigonometricCrossings(AngouriMath.Entity,AngouriMath.Entity.Variable,AngouriMath.Entity.Number.Real,AngouriMath.Entity.Number.Real,System.Boolean)). Their jumps are whole multiples of pi and of
2 pi i / ln(base), and which multiple is read from the values a little either side. A power with an
argument off the line is taken where its base does not cross the negative real axis, since its jump there
is not a constant; a tangent, secant, cotangent or cosecant where its argument does not meet the real line,
where its poles are. Anything else with an argument off the line is not read at all.
