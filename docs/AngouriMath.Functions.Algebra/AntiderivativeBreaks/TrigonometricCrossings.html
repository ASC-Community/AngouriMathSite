TrigonometricCrossings​(AngouriMath.​Entity,​AngouriMath.​Entity.​Variable,​AngouriMath.​Entity.​Number.​Real,​AngouriMath.​Entity.​Number.​Real,​System.​Boolean)

 Method (no overloads)

Summary

The real x in the range where the cosine (cosine) or the sine of
a x + b, with a and b complex numbers, crosses the negative real axis, or
null where the argument is not of that shape, the range is not finite where it would have
to be, or the path meets a zero.

Remarks

With p = Re(a x + b) and q = Im(a x + b), both linear in x,
cos(p + i q) = cos p cosh q - i sin p sinh q is real where sin p = 0 or q = 0: at the
multiples of pi, negative at the odd ones, and at the one point where q = 0, negative where
cos p is there. sin(p + i q) = sin p cosh q + i cos p sinh q is real where cos p = 0 or
q = 0: negative at -pi/2 + 2 k pi, and where sin p is at the point q = 0. A point
where the value touches the axis without crossing it jumps by no multiple, which the caller reads.
