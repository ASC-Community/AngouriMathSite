SolveByWritingAHalfOddPowerOfAReciprocalWithItsSign​(AngouriMath.​Entity,​AngouriMath.​Entity.​Variable,​System.​Boolean)

 Method (no overloads)

Summary

A half-odd power of a constant over a polynomial whose sign changes, among the factors
of the integrand, written apart with the factor that keeps it right on both sides of the
polynomial's zeros: for a real Q other than zero, (A/Q)^r is
K Q^(-r) with K = (A/Q)^r Q^r, whose derivative is zero wherever it has one,
and for a positive number A that is A^r times the sign of Q, the
root of a negative being i times the root of its modulus on both sides of the bar.

Remarks

sqrt(1/(1 - x^2)) is sgn(1 - x^2)/sqrt(1 - x^2), the arcsine's derivative
where it is real, and was declined, with every such power over a polynomial that is not
positive: the reading that writes a power of a quotient apart does so only where it is.
Late in the chain, after the substitutions, which answer some of these without the
factor -- x sqrt(1/(4 - x^2)) is -(1/(4 - x^2))^(-1/2) -- and asked as the
same question, as the sign taken out above is.
