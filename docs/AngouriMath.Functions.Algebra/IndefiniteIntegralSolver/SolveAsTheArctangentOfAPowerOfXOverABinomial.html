SolveAsTheArctangentOfAPowerOfXOverABinomial​(AngouriMath.​Entity,​AngouriMath.​Entity.​Variable,​System.​Boolean)

 Method (no overloads)

Summary

The derivative of w = x^k/L, for a binomial L = x^p + l, over a multiple of
L^2 plus a power of x, c L^2 + d x^(2k): the integrand is
(m/c) w'/(1 + (d/c) w^2), an arctangent of w or a hyperbolic one. Rubi's
1.3.2 (e - 4 f x^3)/(e^2 + 4 d f x^2 + 4 e f x^3 + 4 f^2 x^6) is
arctan(2 sqrt(d f) x/(e + 2 f x^3))/(2 sqrt(d f)), and its rows in symbolic
powers of x are the same in w = x^(m + 1)/(e + 2 f x^n).

Remarks

The denominator is read as four terms, each a constant times a power of x: one is
d x^(2k), and the other three are a + b x^p + c x^(2p) with
b^2 = 4 a c, which is c L^2 for L = x^p + b/(2c). The numerator is
a constant multiple of k x^(k - 1) L - p x^(k + p - 1), which is w' L^2;
the constant is read at x = 1, and the answer is differentiated back. Past a
quartic these were declined: partial fractions over a sextic in x, or over a sum of
symbolic powers of it, are out of reach, and nothing read the quotient.
