SolveATrinomialInTheSquareBesideTheRootOfAQuadratic​(AngouriMath.​Entity,​AngouriMath.​Entity.​Variable)

 Method (no overloads)

Summary

A rational function with a trinomial in the square below the bar, beside a root of a
quadratic, taken apart over the trinomial's roots in the square: a + b x^2 + c x^4 is c (x^2 - r) (x^2 - s) with r, s = (-b -+ sqrt(b^2 - 4 a c))/(2c), and each
factor is a quadratic the rule before this one splits the integrand over and closes
beside the root. Rubi's 1.2.2.4 sqrt(d + e x^2)/(a + b x^2 + c x^4) was declined.

Remarks

Asked with r and s symbols of their own and written back after: the
integrand so written is an integrand for every value of them, and the one asked for at
theirs. Named, each stays one atom through the split: with
(sqrt(b^2 - 4 a c) - b)/(2c) in place of r the split declined
x^2 sqrt(d + e x^2)/(a + b x^2 + c x^4) (#1877 was the
same with a quadratic). The same writing where the roots are a conjugate pair: each
piece over x^2 - r is an arctangent of w x/sqrt(d + e x^2) with
w^2 = (d + e r)/(-r), which holds on either branch of w, and for r off the real line w x/sqrt(d + e x^2) stays off the arctangent's cuts, so the
two conjugate pieces add to a real, continuous antiderivative. Written instead over
the two real quadratics x^2 -+ p x + m, the answer to
x^2 sqrt(d + e x^2)/(a + b x^2 + c x^4) was 29,139 characters where this one is
under 2,000. Not where the discriminant vanishes, the generic case's. With a symbol in
the trinomial only, and in a function of x^2 only.
