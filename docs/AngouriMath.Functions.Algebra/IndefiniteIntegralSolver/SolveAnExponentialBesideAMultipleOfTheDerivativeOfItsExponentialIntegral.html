SolveAnExponentialBesideAMultipleOfTheDerivativeOfItsExponentialIntegral​(AngouriMath.​Entity,​AngouriMath.​Entity.​Variable)

 Method (no overloads)

Summary

An exponential of a rational function h times a rational function R that is
the derivative of e^h N/D and of a multiple of the exponential integral of h:
e^h R = (e^h N/D)' + c e^h h'/h, whose integral is e^h N/D + c Ei(h).
Hebisch's (2 + 2x + 3x^2 - x^3 + 2x^4) e^(x/(2 + x^2))/(2x + x^3) is
(2 + x^2) e^(x/(2 + x^2)) + Ei(x/(2 + x^2)).

Remarks

At a zero r of h of order k where h has no pole, h'/h has
the residue k, and (N/D)' + h' N/D none: a pole of N/D there is one of
order two or more in it. So c is the residue of R at r over k, and
R - c h'/h is asked of SolveByExponentialAnsatz(AngouriMath.Entity,AngouriMath.Entity.Variable)'s ansatz, which
declines R whole: by Liouville's theorem e^h R has no elementary
antiderivative where c is not zero. Each linear factor of the numerator of h,
as written, is a candidate r. The answer is differentiated back at sampled points.
