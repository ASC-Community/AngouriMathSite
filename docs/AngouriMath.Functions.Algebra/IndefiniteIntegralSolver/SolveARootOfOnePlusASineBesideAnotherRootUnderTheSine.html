SolveARootOfOnePlusASineBesideAnotherRootUnderTheSine​(AngouriMath.​Entity,​AngouriMath.​Entity.​Variable)

 Method (no overloads)

Summary

The root of k (1 +- f(y)), or of it over something in f(y), beside another
root of something in f(y), for f a sine or a cosine, under
u = f(y): with g the other function, R = sqrt(k (1 + s f)/D) is
K g/(sqrt(D) sqrt(1 - s f)) where K = R sqrt(D) sqrt(1 - s f)/g has the square
k, and so is constant between the zeros of g; and g dy is
du for the sine and -du for the cosine. What is asked in u is the
rest over sqrt(D(u)) sqrt(1 - s u).

Remarks

The half angle at which 1 +- f is a square reads only roots of that kind, and
Rubi's 4.2.2.1 sqrt(1 - cos(x))/sqrt(a - cos(x)) was declined and
sqrt((1 - cos(x))/(a - cos(x))) ran out of time; under u = cos(x) they are
1/(sqrt(1 + u) sqrt(a - u)), answered in a tenth of a second. Only for a number
k, and where another root of something in the function with a symbol in it is
there: the rules after it answer the rest, and more shortly.
