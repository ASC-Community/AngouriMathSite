SolveAPolynomialTimesAnInverseHyperbolicTangentAndTheLogarithmOfItsQuadratic​(AngouriMath.​Entity,​AngouriMath.​Entity.​Variable)

 Method (no overloads)

Summary

A polynomial in x times a linear combination of 1, T, B and T B,
T = artanh(c x) or arcoth(c x) and B = ln(K (1 - c^2 x^2)), in closed form.
With y = c x, T' = 1/(1 - y^2) and B' = -2 y/(1 - y^2), the integral of
y^j T B is p0 T B + p1 T + p2 B + q1 T^2 + q2 B^2 + r, where
p0 = (y^(j + 1) - [j odd])/(j + 1), q1 = p0(1), q2 = p0(1)/4, and
p1, p2 and r are the integrals of (2 y p0 - 2 q1)/(1 - y^2),
(4 y q2 - p0)/(1 - y^2) and (2 y p2 - p1)/(1 - y^2), polynomials.

Remarks

Matching the coefficients of T B, T, B and 1 asks that the three
numerators vanish at y = 1 and y = -1: the constant of p0 makes the first
two do, and the constants of p1 and p2 the third. Only the derivatives of
T and B are read, which are rational and the same on every branch, so the
answer holds for either inverse function and every K, in the generic case
c != 0. The coefficients are rational in y, and y^k is c^k x^k.
The rules after it ran out of time on Rubi's 7.3.4 and 7.4.1
x^m (a + b artanh(c x))(d + e ln(1 - c^2 x^2)) and the arcoth twins.
Read only where T B is there: without it the rules after it answer.
