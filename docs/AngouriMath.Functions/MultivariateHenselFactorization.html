MultivariateHenselFactorization

← Back to list of classes

Description

Summary

Factorisation in three or more variables by Hensel lifting one variable at a
time, after Wang: factor at a point, lift the factorisation to two variables with
BivariateHenselFactorization, then bring back each remaining variable in
turn.

Remarks

What this adds to the collapse.KroneckerFactorization also reaches three
or more variables, by substituting all the others into one in mixed radix and lifting
the two-variable image. That image's degree is a product of the degrees, and past
what one exponent holds it is refused. Lifting one variable at a time costs a
sum: each variable is lifted to its own degree and no further, so
x ^ 7 - (y + z ^ 3) ^ 7, whose collapsed image has degree 175, is reached here at
degrees 7 and 21.
The lift. The other variables are moved so that the point is the origin:
g = f(x, y_1 + a_1, …, y_k + a_k). The slice g(x, y_1, 0, …, 0) is
factored by the two-variable lift, which also does the recombination, so its factors
are true ones. Then for each further variable y_j the factors are pushed from
agreeing with g at y_j = 0 to agreeing in every power of y_j: the
coefficient of y_j ^ m in the error is written as Σ σ_i · Π_(l≠i) F_l,
with each σ_i of lower degree in x than F_i, and σ_i y_j ^ m is added to F_i. That equation is in the variables already lifted, and it is
solved the same way one variable down, until at the bottom it is in x alone and
the Bezout pairs of the image's factors answer it.
Why a single image factor is a proof. The leading coefficient in x is a
constant and the content in the other variables has been taken out. A factorisation
g = A · B with both of positive degree in x then survives setting any
variable to zero, each side keeping its degree in x; so a slice that does not
factor proves that g does not, and g is the input moved by a ring
automorphism.
A leading coefficient that is a polynomial — Wang's leading-coefficient
problem — is answered the way the two-variable lift answers it, by asking about the
monic L ^ (n - 1) · f(z / L, …) instead; see ByMonicisation(AngouriMath.Functions.MultivariatePolynomial,System.Int32,System.Collections.Generic.List{System.Int32}).
What is refused. A monic form that adds more than
MaxMonicGrowth to the degrees; a polynomial with a repeated factor, whose
images are never square-free; and a slice that splits further than the polynomial
does, for every point tried. The lift cannot follow a factor of the slice that is not
the image of one of the polynomial, so it finds that the product it built is not the
polynomial and the point is given up. Every factor is checked by exact division before
it is returned.
