 Method (no overloads)

Summary

What the jumps of antiderivative where it crosses a branch cut add to F(b) - F(a) over
[lower, upper], or null where one cannot be
settled.

Remarks

A node that jumps by J at t0 and stands in the antiderivative with the coefficient c makes it jump by c(t0) J, which F(b) - F(a) counts and the integral does not. At a bound on the
cut, the antiderivative's value there is the one on the cut and the integral's is the one from inside the
range, and they differ by the part of the jump on that side. So 1/(1 + (t - 3i)^2) over [-2, 2] is arctan(2 - 3i) - arctan(-2 - 3i) - pi, and over the line 0.
